## Preservation and limits

The original Tiled import, LDtk, hexagonal/staggered overhang, editable-map navigation, worker terrain, collision basics, kinematic movement, Tiled collision actor and joint-shadow donor sources remain present and unchanged. Their guide destinations and central aliases belong to the foundation integration. This batch does not certify every donor interaction solely from preserving its source; the main targets and the named worker/streamer regression surfaces have the concrete coverage listed above.

Real gamepad and touch hardware are NOT_TESTED. Keyboard and pointer browser automation are software evidence. Firefox WebGPU was not attempted because the available environment has no adapter. No new artwork, dependencies or asset downloads are included; final character artwork remains outside this batch. Full repository/push gates and PR publication are not performed by this worker.
